# CS702 — Research Methodology — PLAN

**Semester:** 7 · **Credits:** 2 · **Level:** Advanced-Professional
**Theory:** 30 hrs · **Practice:** 15 hrs · **Tools:** LaTeX, Zotero/Mendeley, reference managers, plagiarism checkers

## Syllabus

### Unit 1 — What Research Is (6 lectures)
1. Research vs engineering — the difference in goals
2. Types of research — exploratory, descriptive, explanatory, applied
3. The research process — question, method, evidence, conclusion
4. Formulating a research question — FINER criteria
5. Hypothesis formulation and testing
6. Research ethics — honesty, attribution, consent

### Unit 2 — Literature Review (6 lectures)
1. Why a literature review matters
2. Systematic vs narrative reviews
3. Searching — databases, keywords, boolean operators
4. Reading a paper — the three-pass method
5. Note-taking and synthesis — comparison matrices
6. Identifying gaps and positioning your work

### Unit 3 — Research Methods (6 lectures)
1. Quantitative methods — experiments, benchmarks, surveys
2. Qualitative methods — interviews, case studies, ethnography
3. Mixed methods
4. Designing an experiment — variables, controls, baselines
5. Reproducibility and replication
6. Bias, validity, and reliability in research

### Unit 4 — Data Analysis and Writing (6 lectures)
1. Descriptive statistics for research
2. Inferential statistics — choosing the right test
3. Visualising results honestly
4. Structure of a paper — abstract, intro, related work, method, results, discussion, conclusion
5. Writing style for research — precision, hedging, tense
6. Citations and reference management — IEEE, APA, ACM

### Unit 5 — Publishing and Review (6 lectures)
1. Venues — journals, conferences, workshops, preprints
2. The peer review process
3. Responding to reviews
4. Open access and preprints (arXiv, SSRN)
5. Ethics of authorship — order, contribution, ghostwriting
6. Predatory journals — how to spot them

## Practical Labs (15)

1. Read one paper and write a one-page summary
2. Three-pass method — apply it to two papers
3. Literature search — find 10 papers in a chosen area
4. Build a synthesis matrix — 10 papers × 5 dimensions
5. Identify a research gap — a one-page writeup
6. Write a research question using the FINER criteria
7. Design an experiment — variables and controls
8. Compute descriptive statistics on a dataset
9. Choose and run the right statistical test on real data
10. Write an abstract for your capstone
11. Write an introduction for your capstone
12. LaTeX — typeset a one-page research note
13. Reference management — Zotero with IEEE style
14. Peer review — critique a classmate's abstract
15. Respond to peer review — revise your abstract

## Assignments

1. Literature search report — 10 papers with summaries
2. Research question and hypothesis formulation
3. Experiment design — variables, methods, evaluation plan
4. Written paper draft — 4 pages, LaTeX formatted
5. Peer review and revision report
